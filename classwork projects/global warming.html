THE GLOBAL CRISIS

Understanding Global Warming

Global warming refers to the long-term rise in Earth's average temperature. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation and industrial pollution release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. These gases trap additional heat and make our planet warmer. This is one of the biggest environmental challenges facing humanity today.

WHAT IS DESTROYING OUR PLANET?

Four major causes of global warming

🏭

Industrial Pollution

Factories release greenhouse gases that contribute to global warming.

🚘

Vehicle Emissions

Vehicles release carbon dioxide by burning petrol and diesel.

🌳

Deforestation

Cutting down forests reduces nature's ability to absorb carbon dioxide.

🔥

Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil and natural gas releases greenhouse gases.

THE WARNING SIGNS

How global warming affects our world

🌡️

Extreme Heat

Rising temperatures increase the frequency and intensity of heatwaves.

🧊

Melting Ice

Glaciers and ice sheets are losing ice as the planet warms.

🌊

Rising Oceans

Rising sea levels threaten coastal communities and ecosystems.

🌪️

Climate Disasters

Climate change increases the risks of several extreme weather events.
