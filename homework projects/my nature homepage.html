Welcome to Nature Explorer

Nature is full of beautiful plants, animals and amazing places. Let's explore some of them!

🌳 Forests

Forests are home to many plants, birds and animals. They also help keep our environment healthy.

  • Many trees
  • Wild animals
  • Fresh air

💧 Rivers

Rivers provide water to people, animals and plants. They are an important part of nature.

  1. Source of water
  2. Home for aquatic life
  3. Useful for farming

🌸 Flowers

Flowers make our surroundings colourful and provide food for insects such as bees and butterflies.

  • Beautiful colours
  • Sweet fragrance
  • Help plants reproduce

🌍 Protect Nature

We should protect nature by planting trees, saving water, reducing pollution and keeping our surroundings clean.

Small actions can make a big difference!
