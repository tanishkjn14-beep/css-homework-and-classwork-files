What is global warming?

Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature caused primarily by human activities that release heat-trapping gases into the atmosphere. How It Works (The Greenhouse Effect) • Solar Radiation: Sunlight reaches Earth, and the surface absorbs that energy and re-radiates it as heat. • Trapped Heat: Gases like carbon dioxide (\(CO_{2}\)), methane (\(CH_{4}\)), and nitrous oxide (\(N_{2}O\)) act like a thermal blanket in the atmosphere. • Enhanced Warming: Human actions have thickened this blanket, trapping more heat and raising global temperatures.
